# suppl. privacy policy (draft)

> Draft for legal review before release. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

suppl. reminds you to take the supplements you already use. It is built so your health information stays on your iPhone.

## What suppl. stores
- The supplements you add (name, brand, dose, timing, notes) and the times you tell it about your day.
- What you do with reminders (Taken, Snooze, Skip) and a short history of recent reminders and why they were sent.

This is stored only on your device. suppl. has no accounts and no server that receives this information.

## Connected apps (optional)
- **Apple Health**: if you connect it, suppl. reads your workouts and sleep to time reminders after a workout or after you wake up. It does not write to Apple Health. This data is never sent off your device and is never used for advertising.
- **Calendar**: if you connect it, suppl. reads only when you are busy (start and end times). It never reads event titles, notes, locations, or attendees.
- **WHOOP**: if you connect it, your phone talks to WHOOP directly to read workouts and sleep. A small token service operated for suppl. exchanges WHOOP login codes for access tokens. It does not store tokens or any health data.

You can disconnect any of these in Profile at any time.

## Analytics and advertising
suppl. contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing you enter is sold or shared.

## Subscriptions
Purchases are handled by Apple. suppl. sees whether you have an active subscription, not your payment details.

## Deleting your data
Profile > Delete all data removes your saved routine, reminder history, settings, and WHOOP sign-in from this device, and disconnects connected apps. If saved routine deletion fails, the app reports the failure so you can retry.

Uninstalling removes the app's local routine and history, but iOS Keychain sign-in credentials can survive uninstall. Use Delete all data before uninstalling to clear the WHOOP sign-in. On a fresh reinstall, suppl. also clears an old WHOOP sign-in once protected device storage is available. Only you can revoke Health or Calendar permission in iOS settings.

## Medical disclaimer
suppl. doesn't give medical advice. Talk to a pharmacist or doctor about doses and interactions.
